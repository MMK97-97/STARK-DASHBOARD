Calculation standard

U.S. parcel screening uses a conservative 150 lb, 108 in longest-side and 165 in length-plus-girth limit. Shipping-box dimensions are rounded up before DIM-weight screening; billable weight is the greater of rounded actual or dimensional weight. Pallet calculations test carton orientations, layer utilization, loaded height and weight. Freight class uses the 2025 NMFC density scale and must still be verified against the commodity's current NMFC item and carrier tariff.
