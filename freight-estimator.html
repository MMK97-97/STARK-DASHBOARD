How this estimator works

Parcel calculations round dimensions up and compare actual weight with dimensional weight. UPS Daily and FedEx use a 139 divisor; UPS Retail can use 166. UPS screening uses 150 lb, 108-inch length and 165-inch length-plus-girth limits. FedEx screening uses 150 lb and 165-inch length-plus-girth limits, with oversize alerts above 96 inches in length or 130 inches length-plus-girth. Freight planning uses a 48 × 40-inch pallet, configurable height and weight limits, density-based LTL class guidance and a 26-pallet/45,000-lb dry-van planning reference. This is a planning estimate, not a carrier rate quote or final NMFC classification.
