Calculation standard

Package dimensions are rounded up before DIM and size screening. Billable weight is the greater of rounded actual or dimensional weight. UPS screening uses 150 lb, 108-inch maximum length and 165-inch length-plus-girth. FedEx screening uses 150 lb and 165-inch length-plus-girth, with oversize exposure above 96 inches in length or 130 inches length-plus-girth. Pallet calculations use the selected footprint, actual carton layer fit, loaded-height limit and pallet-weight limit. LTL freight class is density guidance only.
