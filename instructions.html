Reorder rules

  • Included statuses: Live, Fashion and Backorder.
  • Excluded statuses: Feeds Only, Internal Use and Presentation.
  • Only brands marked Active are included.
  • Reorder when Stock Available plus Open Supplier Qty is at or below the critical threshold.
  • Reorder when open client orders exceed available stock.
  • Reorder when average monthly sales exceed available stock plus open supplier quantity.
  • Recommended Reorder Qty equals average monthly sales multiplied by coverage months.

ABC rules

Class A covers the first 80% of cumulative sales, Class B covers 80%–95%, and Class C contains the remaining items.

Expected source columns

Brand, Item ID, Model#, Item Title, Status, ETA, Vol Past 3M, Vol Last 30 Days, Avg/PerM Past 3M, Stock Qty, Stock Available, Open Orders From Client and Open Orders To Supplier.
