Reorder Rules

  • Included statuses are Live, Fashion and Backorder.
  • Feeds Only, Internal Use and Presentation are excluded.
  • Only brands marked Active are included.
  • Reorder when Stock Available plus Open Supplier Qty is at or below the configured critical threshold.
  • Reorder when open client orders exceed Stock Available.
  • Reorder when the latest supplier delivery exceeds the configured delay.
  • Reorder when average monthly sales exceed Stock Available plus Open Supplier Qty.
  • Recommended Reorder Qty is calculated from average monthly sales and the configured inventory settings.

ABC Rules

Items are ranked by Vol Past 3M. Class A covers the first 80% of cumulative sales units, Class B covers 80% through 95%, and Class C contains the remaining items. Thresholds can be changed on the Dashboard.

Expected Source Columns

Brand, itemid, Model#, Item Title, Status, ETA, Vol Past 3M, Vol Last 30 Days, Avg/PerM Past 3M, Stock Qty, Stock Available, STOCK difference, OPEN ORDERS FROM CLIENT and OPEN ORDERS TO SUPPLIER.
