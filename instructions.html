Inventory workflow

Follow these four steps for each regional report.

01

Upload Raw Report

Upload the regional Average Item Sales Report. The source file remains stored in the browser for that region.

02

Review Active Brands

Activate the required brands and maintain an editable supplier lead time for each brand.

03

Save Calculation Rules

Adjust the critical stock, supplier-delay and ABC thresholds for the selected region.

04

Review Reorder Report

Use the header filters, review recommended quantities and export the final report.

Expected source columns

The Raw Report should contain the following fields for accurate inventory and reorder calculations.

Brand itemid Model# Item Title Status ETA Vol Past 3M Vol Last 30 Days Avg/PerM Past 3M Stock Qty Stock Available STOCK difference OPEN ORDERS FROM CLIENT OPEN ORDERS TO SUPPLIER

Separate regional instructions

US, EU and Canada maintain separate calculation values. Saving instructions for United States will not change the settings used by the other regions.
