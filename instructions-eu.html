Reorder rules

  • Included statuses are Live, Fashion and Backorder.
  • Excluded statuses are Feeds Only, Internal Use and Presentation.
  • Only brands marked Active are included.
  • Reorder when Stock Available plus Open Supplier Quantity is at or below the critical threshold.
  • Reorder when open client orders exceed stock available.
  • Reorder when the latest supplier delivery exceeds the configured delay.
  • Reorder when average monthly sales exceed Stock Available plus Open Supplier Quantity.
  • Recommended Reorder Quantity = ((On Hand + Open Supplier Quantity − Open Orders From Client) × Lead Time from Active Brands) + Avg/Month.
  • Negative recommended quantities are set to zero and results are rounded up.

ATS rules

  • ATS records are stored separately for the European Union.
  • Each ATS record contains a Model# and Quantity.
  • Import files may be XLS, XLSX, CSV or TSV.
  • ATS import files should contain Model# and Quantity columns.
  • When the same model is imported again, its previous ATS quantity is replaced.

ABC rules

Items are ranked by Vol Past 3M. Class A covers the first 80% of cumulative sales units, Class B covers 80%–95%, and Class C contains the remaining items. Thresholds can be changed on the Dashboard.

Expected source columns

Brand, itemid, Model#, Item Title, Status, ETA, Vol Past 3M, Vol Last 30 Days, Avg/PerM Past 3M, Stock Qty, Stock Available, STOCK difference, OPEN ORDERS FROM CLIENT and OPEN ORDERS TO SUPPLIER.
